What This Means

Herndon home values are holding steady even as homes take longer to sell than they did a year ago — a sign the market is normalizing after several fast-paced years, not cooling off. More buyers are closing deals month over month, but sellers now need better pricing and presentation to compete, rather than counting on a quick sale at any price.
