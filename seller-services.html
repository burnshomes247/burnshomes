Seller Services

29 years and $500 million in closed transactions have taught me exactly what moves a sale — and what doesn't.

Every seller I work with gets the same thing: a straightforward, experienced read on where your home stands and what it will take to sell it well. I've priced, prepared, and marketed homes across nearly every kind of market this area has seen — and I bring that judgment to every listing, not a script.

Below is a quick look at the factors I weigh most closely. It's a glimpse, not the whole picture — the real value is in how these pieces come together for your specific property, which is best discussed directly.

Property Price

Getting the number right against recent sales, active competition, and where the market is trending — not just today, but by the time you close.

Property Location

Neighborhood, schools, walkability, and proximity to transit all shape buyer demand in ways that are often underestimated.

Property Condition

Updates, layout, curb appeal, and parking — and knowing which of these are worth addressing before you list.

Property Presentation

How your home is marketed and timed often matters as much as the home itself. This is where experience shows up most.
